A SMALL SETUP, THEN YOU’RE OFF

Ready for next semester?

A userscript manager adds the helper to the pages where you need it.

  1. 1

    Get a userscript manager.

    Already have one? You’re set. Otherwise, choose Tampermonkey ↗ or Violentmonkey ↗ and follow its browser setup.

  2. 2

    Install the helper.

    Click the button, review the script in your manager, and confirm Install. Your browser always leaves that final choice to you.

  3. 3

    Head back to BroncoDirect.

    Refresh Class Search or Add Classes. The helper appears in the page. If you used the old extension, disable it first.

Install the user script ↗

A few things to know.

Your schedule stays on the page.

Cart and enrolled-class details are read in your browser. They aren’t uploaded by the helper. Subject preferences and cached results live in your userscript manager’s storage. Professor-name lookups go to Rate My Professors without browser cookies.

Course suggestions aren’t a promise of open seats.

The included directory is a snapshot of 4,166 course names across 90 subjects, combining archived catalog names with newer listings. Some older courses remain, and ATH, CM, SME, and OAPL have no directory records. BroncoDirect remains the place to check availability and requirements.

A missing professor rating doesn’t mean a bad rating.

Profiles must match the professor’s name and CPP campus uniquely. Missing or ambiguous profiles and unavailable lookups show N/A, with a link to search within CPP.

The install button opened a page full of code.

Your manager may be missing, disabled, or waiting for browser permission. Enable it, follow its userscript setup prompt, and try again. You can also create a new script in the manager and paste in the downloaded file.
